## Purpose and scope

This procedure explains how the warehouse team performs shelf-life and quality check for the Snack foods category. It applies to the SKUs listed in the document metadata and to any pack size derived from them.

## Steps

1. Check remaining shelf life against the category minimum before offering the batch.
2. Quarantine any batch with an unclear date code until the supplier owner confirms it.
3. Separate damaged or leaking packs at the quality point and record the quantity.

## Records

The operator records the state version, the quantity, the reason code and the approver for every exception. Records are retained with the intake or dispatch reference so a reviewer can reconstruct the action.

## Escalation
