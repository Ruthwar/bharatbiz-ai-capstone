# Case Note - Substitution Refusal in Secunderabad (Snack foods)

SIMULATION ONLY. This is a synthetic case note written for the BharatBiz academic capstone. It is not a real distributor policy, supplier agreement or regulatory extract. Every party, amount and reference is fictional. This document is subordinate to the distributor-wide policy; where the two appear to differ the distributor-wide policy prevails.

## Situation

A retailer refused a five litre tin offered in place of a one litre pouch during a shortage. The case involved snack foods lines and retailer RET-020 in Secunderabad.

## What was checked

The desk checked that consent had not been recorded and returned the line to the original product.

## Outcome

The refusal was recorded, the line was re-planned and the reviewer saw the original product on the card. The reviewer recorded the decision and the reason against the current state version.

## Lesson

A pack-size change is a substitution and needs recorded consent.
