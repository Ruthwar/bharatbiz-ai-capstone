## Situation

A retailer disputed an invoice because the rate was higher than the figure quoted the previous week. The case involved biscuits lines and retailer RET-015 in Kukatpally.

## What was checked

The desk compared the invoice date with the version of the rate card in force that day.

## Outcome

The new rate applied from its written effective date and the invoice was explained with the card reference. The reviewer recorded the decision and the reason against the current state version.

## Lesson

A verbal price quote never overrides the approved rate card.
