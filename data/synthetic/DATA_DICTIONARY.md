# Synthetic dataset dictionary

Generated by `scripts/generate_synthetic_data.py`; all entities and events are fictional. Reproducible with a fixed seed. Currency is exact decimal rupees with two decimal places; quantities use the explicit `unit`/`base_unit` field. Dates are ISO 8601; timestamps include +05:30. The live demo state is dated 2026-11-23 (`synthetic-v3-live-20261123`). `messages.jsonl` contains 1,000 labelled synthetic examples; `events.jsonl` has corresponding evidence-linked event annotations. Keep both separate from any permissioned pilot data. `scenarios.csv/json` provide fourteen repeatable benchmark cases; `scenario_fixtures/` adds the `SCN-009` variant for fifteen executable fixtures. Supplier offers are valid only for listed supplier-SKU rows. No value represents verified local prices or business activity.

Supplier and retailer rows carry fictional trade names (`supplier_name`, `retailer_name`, `shop_type`, locality `area`). Names are display labels only; `supplier_id` and `retailer_id` remain the internal join keys used by orders, deliveries, facts, plans and review records. Messages quote trade names the way a person would write them, while the `entities` annotation stores both the name and the matching ID.

Tables: products, suppliers, retailers, supplier_products, inventory, historical_order_lines, open_orders, supplier_deliveries, scheduled_receivables, retailer_complaints, market_observations, cash_ledger, scenarios and messages/events. Stable IDs, explicit units, two-decimal INR strings and state_version values connect related rows. Unknown source facts must remain unknown.

Extended tables: `scheduled_receivables` uses `receivable_id` with a `retailer_id` foreign key and exact INR amounts; `retailer_complaints` uses `complaint_id` with `retailer_id` and `sku_id` foreign keys; `market_observations` uses `observation_id` and `source_id` with retailer/SKU scope, explicit uncertainty and corroboration status. All three carry the business state's `state_version` and `source_type: synthetic`.

Advisory review tables: `retailer_credit_limits` stores the demo credit limit and payment terms per retailer; `trade_schemes` stores approved scheme terms with validity and minimum quantity; `delivery_evidence` reconciles ordered, received, damaged and wrong-item units to an invoice and proof of delivery; `batch_lots` stores batch expiry and supplier return-window terms; `recall_notices` links a recall to a batch and required action; `dispatch_routes` and `dispatch_orders` define vehicle capacity and route demand. These tables are versioned synthetic inputs for review only; the application never mutates them.
