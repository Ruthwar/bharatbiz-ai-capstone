# Retailer and Supplier Communication Policy

SIMULATION ONLY. Synthetic policy for an academic capstone. The system described here sends nothing to a real party.

## 1. Two acknowledgements, both templates

| Kind | When it is sent | What it says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Receipt | On receipt of a valid message | "Received. It will be reviewed." |
| Complete | When the required details are all present | "All details noted. It will be processed." |

Both are fixed templates. Neither is generated by a model.

## 2. Rules

**R1 - An acknowledgement asserts nothing.** It contains no date, no quantity, no price, no order reference and no promise. Digits and currency symbols are excluded by design, because a number in an acknowledgement reads as a commitment.

**R2 - Receipt is not processability.** A message that cannot be processed still receives a receipt. The processing problem is recorded for triage; the sender is not told a decision has been made.

**R3 - Duplicates are acknowledged once.** A repeated message id is recorded as a duplicate and not acknowledged a second time.

**R4 - Language follows the sender.** Templates exist for English, Hindi and Hinglish. The acknowledgement uses the language detected on the verified sender's message; it is not translated by a model at send time.

**R5 - No substantive message leaves the system.** Any reply that discusses a date, quantity, price, credit, substitute or outcome requires human approval and is sent outside this system. The acknowledgements above are the only exception.

**R6 - Nothing is executed by approval.** Approving a plan updates a demo record. It does not raise a purchase order, move money, change stock or notify a supplier or retailer.

**R7 - Wrong party, no processing.** If the sender's identity does not match the party the message claims to speak for, the message is acknowledged as received and routed to human triage. An acknowledgement must not confirm that the claimed party was believed.

## 3. Why the restriction is deliberate

A distributor that auto-replies "your order will arrive tomorrow" has made a commercial promise from an unverified message. The cost of being wrong is a lost retailer, not a failed test. Template acknowledgements keep the receipt useful and the commitment with a human.

## 4. Escalation wording

Triage notes are internal. They may say "supplier identity mismatch" or "revised date not confirmed". They are never sent to the counterparty as-is.
