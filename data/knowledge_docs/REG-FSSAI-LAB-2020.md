# FSSAI Labelling and Display Regulations 2020 - Extract

Source: Food Safety and Standards (Labelling and Display) Regulations, 2020, Version VIII dated 09.09.2025, published by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India. Extracted 2026-11-13. Verbatim quotations are marked; everything outside a quotation is commentary by this project and is not the Authority's text. This extract is a study aid, not legal advice.

## 1. What the regulation covers

Regulation 1(2): the regulations "prescribe the labelling requirements of pre-packaged foods and display of essential information on premises where food is manufactured, processed, served and stored."

Regulation 1(3) states commencement on gazette publication, with a one-year compliance period for food business operators and a later date for Chapter 3. A compliance direction dated 22 October 2021 moved compliance to 1 July 2022.

## 2. General requirements

Regulation 4(3) prohibits a label that is "false, misleading or deceptive or is likely to create an erroneous impression regarding its character in any respect."

Regulation 4(5): "The particulars of declaration required under these Regulations to be specified on the label shall be in English or Hindi in Devnagri script". Other languages may be added, provided they do not contradict.

Regulation 4(7) requires label contents to be "clear, unambiguous, prominent, conspicuous, indelible and readily legible by the consumer under normal conditions of purchase and use".

## 3. Particulars required on the label

Regulation 5 lists the particulars. The ones that matter for a retail pack in this catalogue:

- **Name of the food** on the front of pack, indicating its true nature (Regulation 5(1)).
- **List of ingredients**, except for single-ingredient foods, in descending order of weight or volume (Regulation 5(2)). Where an edible vegetable oil is an ingredient, the label must "Give name of the specific edible oil such as mustard oil, groundnut oil, etc." (Regulation 5(2) table).
- **Name and complete address.** Regulation 5(6)(a): "The name and complete address of the brand owner, whether or not, he himself is the manufacturer, marketer, packer or bottler, as the case may be, shall be declared on the label." It must be preceded by a qualifying phrase such as "Manufactured by", "Marketed by" or "Packed & Marketed by".
- **FSSAI logo and licence number.** Regulation 5(7)(a): "The FSSAI logo and license number under the Act shall be displayed on the label of the food package in contrast color to the background". Where the manufacturer's licence differs from the brand owner's, both are displayed (Regulation 5(7)(b)).
- **Net quantity, retail sale price and consumer care details.** Regulation 5(8): "Declaration and manner of declaration of Net quantity, Retail Sale Price and Consumer Care details shall be as provided in Legal Metrology Act, 2009 (1 of 2010) and the Rules made there under." This is the bridge to REG-LMPC-2011-R6.
- **Batch identification.** Regulation 5(9): "A batch number or code number or lot number shall be declared on the label."
- **Date marking.** Regulation 5(10)(a): "'Date of manufacture or packaging' and 'Expiry/Use by' shall be declared on the label." "Best before" may be used as optional or additional information. The format depends on shelf life: DD/MM/YY for short shelf life, month and year for longer shelf life.

## 4. Why this project cites it

The catalogue treats FSSAI labelling as a supplier obligation, not a planning input. When a message proposes a different pack, the substitution rule (POL-SUB-003) requires the alternative to be a compliant pack with a declared net quantity and licence. The extract is retrieved so a reviewer can see the requirement behind that check rather than taking it on trust.

The regulation also explains why a pack size is a legal declaration: net quantity is not an internal convenience, it is what the consumer is told. That is why the system will not convert 1 L pouches into 5 L tins and call it the same order.

## 5. Limits of this extract

Only the provisions relevant to a retail food pack were copied. Nutrition information, front-of-pack warnings, vegan and non-vegetarian marks, allergen declarations, fortification and advertising claims are outside this extract and outside the demo's scope. Check the current version on the FSSAI site before relying on any quotation.
