# Market-Signal Collection and Corroboration SOP

SIMULATION ONLY. Synthetic operating procedure for an academic capstone.

## 1. Trigger

Use this procedure when a market observation may affect a demand simulation, supply-risk review or prioritisation decision.

## 2. Procedure

1. Create a signal id and record source, date, geography, SKU/category and exact observation.
2. Classify the signal as demand, supply, price, competitor, seasonality, event or other.
3. Check whether the source is a duplicate, forwarded copy or already represented in the corpus.
4. Identify at least one independent source relevant to the same scope and time window.
5. Compare direction, magnitude, timing and scope; record agreement and conflict explicitly.
6. Assign corroborated, single-source, conflicting or stale status with a reason.
7. If useful, create a separately labelled scenario assumption. Do not edit baseline orders or stock.
8. Route any recommendation that changes a plan or creates a financial exposure to human review.
9. Set a review or expiry date and retain the underlying evidence.

## 3. Review checklist

The reviewer confirms independence, date, scope, exact evidence, duplicate handling, uncertainty and the separation between the signal and the scenario assumption.

## 4. Prohibited shortcuts

- Counting social forwards as independent corroboration.
- Converting a market comment directly into an order quantity.
- Using an undated or expired signal as current evidence.
- Hiding conflicting signals because they make the scenario inconvenient.
