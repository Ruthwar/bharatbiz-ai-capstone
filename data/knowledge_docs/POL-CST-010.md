# Procurement Cost Discipline Policy

SIMULATION ONLY. This is a synthetic policy written for an academic capstone. It is not a real distributor policy. All suppliers, products and amounts are fictional.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy guides advisory recommendations when multiple eligible supplier or stock plans can serve the same demand. It encourages prudent use of working capital without overriding service, safety or affordability requirements.

## 2. Rules

**R1 - Consider total plan cost.** Compare the verified total procurement cost for each eligible plan, not an isolated unit rate. The review must also show the resulting cash headroom under POL-CSH-002 and POL-LIQ-007.

**R2 - Avoid paying a premium without a service reason.** When two eligible plans provide materially similar fulfilment, lateness and arrival outcomes, prefer the lower-cost plan. When a higher-cost plan is considered, explain the verified service benefit that the additional cost provides.

**R3 - Keep affordability as a hard gate.** Cost discipline does not permit a plan to breach the approved minimum cash buffer, supplier terms or other mandatory controls. Such a plan is excluded before recommendation, regardless of its service outcome.

**R4 - Do not assume future receipts or supplier credit.** Use only the cash treatment approved in the versioned state and POL-CSH-002. Expected collections and unapproved payment arrangements do not make a plan affordable.

**R5 - Do not optimize cost by changing the ordered product.** A lower-cost pack or SKU is not an equivalent fulfilment plan unless POL-SUB-003 and documented retailer consent permit the substitution.

## 3. Review output

When eligible plans differ in cost, show the rupee difference, each plan's verified service outcomes, and cash headroom. The recommendation explains whether the extra cost buys a documented service improvement or whether the lower-cost plan provides materially similar service.

## 4. Audit

The recommendation records the state version, candidate ids, total costs and cash projections used in the comparison. A changed price, quantity, payment term or cash position requires a new calculation and review under POL-PRC-005.
