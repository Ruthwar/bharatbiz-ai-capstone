# Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules 2011 - Rule 6 Extract

Source: The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 (Union of India), consolidated text as published on Indian Kanoon, retrieved 2026-11-13. The Government of India copy of the Rules is also published by the Department of Consumer Affairs and by State weights and measures departments; the copy on the Meghalaya department site is a scanned image and could not be text-searched. Verbatim quotations are marked. Commentary outside quotation marks is this project's, not the Government's.

## 1. When declarations are required

Rule 4 provides that no person shall pre-pack a commodity for sale unless the package bears the required declarations. Rule 5 deals with standard pack sizes for specified commodities.

## 2. Rule 6(1) - the declaration

"Every package shall bear thereon or on label securely affixed thereto, a definite, plain and conspicuous declaration made in accordance with the provisions of this chapter".

The declaration covers:

- **(a) Name and address.** "the name and address of the manufacturer, or where the manufacturer is not the packer, the name and address of the manufacturer and packer and for any imported package the name and address of the importer". For food packages, Explanation III directs that the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 applies to this clause instead.
- **(b) Common or generic name.** "The common or generic names of the commodity contained in the package", and where there is more than one product, the name and number or quantity of each.
- **(c) Net quantity.** "The net quantity, in terms of the standard unit of weight or measure, of the commodity contained in the package or where the commodity is packed or sold by number, the number of the commodity contained in the package".
- **(d) Month and year of manufacture or packing.** The month and year in which the commodity is manufactured, pre-packed or imported. For food, the Food Safety and Standards Act and rules apply instead.
- **(e) Retail sale price.** The price must "clearly indicate that it is the maximum retail price inclusive of all taxes and the price in rupees and paise be rounded off to the nearest rupee or 50 paise". Permitted forms include "MRP Rs. xx.xx (inclusive of all taxes)".
- **(f) Dimensions** where the size of the commodity is relevant.

## 3. Rule 6(2) - consumer care

"Every package shall bear the name, address, telephone number, E-mail address of the person who can be or the office which can be or the office which can be contacted, in case of consumer complaints."

## 4. Rule 6(3) - stickers

Individual stickers may not be affixed to alter or make a required declaration. The stated exception is a sticker showing a **reduced** maximum retail price, provided it does not cover the original MRP declaration. This is why a price revision downward can be stickered and a price revision upward cannot.

## 5. Rule 7 - display panel

Rule 7 governs the principal display panel and the minimum height of numerals and letters in the declarations. The principal display panel is the part of the package most likely to be seen by the consumer at the point of sale.

## 6. Why this project cites it

Two practical consequences for a distributor:

1. **A pack is not interchangeable with another pack size.** Net quantity is a declared legal characteristic, not an internal packaging choice. This supports POL-SUB-003.
2. **Price changes have a physical form.** A downward MRP revision may be stickered; an upward revision may not. A supplier re-quote therefore has a compliance dimension, not only a cost dimension. See POL-PRC-005.

The extract contains no rule that authorises a distributor to change a supplier's declaration. The obligation sits with the manufacturer, packer or importer; the distributor's check is that the pack carries the declaration on receipt.

## 7. Limits of this extract

Rule 6 is copied in part. Rules on wholesale packages, exemptions, e-commerce declarations, imports and penalties are outside this extract. The consolidated copy may not include the most recent amendments; verify against the Gazette before relying on a quotation.
