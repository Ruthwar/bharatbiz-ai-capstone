# GST Rates for the Catalogue - Schedule as on 22.09.2025

Sources retrieved 2026-11-13:

- GST Council, 56th meeting recommendations, PIB release dated 03.09.2025: https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/press_release_press_information_bureau_0.pdf
- CGST rate schedule "as on 22.09.2025", Notification No. 09/2025-Central Tax (Rate) dated 17.09.2025, compiled by the Commercial Taxes Department, Government of Jammu and Kashmir: https://jkcomtax.gov.in/pdf/GST%20rates2025.pdf

The Council release describes the reform as "a 2 rate structure with a Standard Rate of 18% and a Merit Rate of 5%; a special de-merit rate of 40% for a select few goods and services". The compilation lists CGST rates of 2.5%, 9% and 20%, which correspond to combined GST of 5%, 18% and 40%.

The schedule is a reference compilation. Its own disclaimer states that only notifications published in the official Gazette have the force of law. Verify the current notification before invoicing.

## 2. The lines that need care

**Rice is rate-split by presentation, not by grain.** The schedule places "Rice, pre-packaged and labelled" at 5%, and rice "other than pre-packaged and labelled" at nil. A distributor selling both loose and packed rice has two rates in one SKU family.

**Soap is rate-split by type.** "Toilet Soap (other than industrial soap) in the form of bars, cakes, moulded pieces or shapes" is at 5%. Soap and organic surface-active products other than toilet soap in those forms are at 18%. A generic "soap" line on a purchase order does not settle the rate.

**Beverages split three ways.** Fruit-pulp or fruit-juice based drinks other than carbonated beverages are at 5%, beverages containing milk at 5%, carbonated beverages of fruit drink or with fruit juice at 40%, and caffeinated beverages at 40%. The catalogue's SKU-010 "Beverage" is not specific enough to assign a rate without the supplier's HSN and product description.

**Detergent stays at the standard rate.** Washing preparations under 3402 are at 18%, not the merit rate. The 5% concession in the Council release covers toilet soap bars, shampoo and toothpaste, not laundry detergent.

## 3. Why this project cites it

The planner does not compute GST. Costs in the synthetic state are unit costs, and the demo's cash projection treats procurement cost as an immediate outflow (POL-CSH-002). The rate table is retrieved for two reasons:

1. A reviewer checking a substitution needs to know that the landed cost of a different pack can change even when the supplier's rate looks similar.
2. It is a worked example of the rule that retrieval supplies text, never numbers (POL-DAT-004 R10). The table is a reference, not an input to the arithmetic. If tax were modelled, it would come from the state, dated, not from a retrieved paragraph.

## 4. Currency of this extract

Rates are stated as on 22.09.2025 and were retrieved on 2026-11-13. No amendment after 22.09.2025 was checked for this extract. A rate change is a price change under POL-PRC-005 and must be dated in the business state before it can affect a plan.
