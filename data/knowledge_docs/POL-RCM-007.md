# Retailer Complaints and Resolution Policy

SIMULATION ONLY. Synthetic policy for an academic capstone. It does not create a real customer-service commitment.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy governs the intake, classification, evidence handling and human resolution of retailer complaints about availability, delay, quantity, quality, price or communication.

## 2. Rules

**R1 - Acknowledge without promising.** A receipt acknowledgement confirms intake only. It must not promise a refund, delivery date, replacement or credit.

**R2 - Preserve the complaint.** Store the original message, verified sender, timestamp, order reference if present and exact evidence spans.

**R3 - Separate fact from allegation.** The complaint is recorded as the retailer's report until supporting state, dispatch or count evidence is checked.

**R4 - One case, one owner.** A complaint receives a case id, severity, owner, next action and due review time. Duplicates link to the original case.

**R5 - Safety and compliance first.** A product safety, labelling, payment or personal-data concern is escalated immediately under the applicable control and is not resolved by a model.

**R6 - Human approval for remedy.** Refunds, credits, substitutions, revised dates and substantive replies require human approval and remain outside this simulation.

**R7 - No retaliation or suppression.** A complaint must not reduce service priority, delete history or alter stock, price or order records.

## 3. Resolution states

Cases are labelled received, investigating, awaiting evidence, resolved or escalated. Resolved means the decision and evidence are recorded; it does not mean the retailer accepted the outcome.

## 4. Audit

The case history retains the original complaint, evidence, owner changes, decision, approver and closure reason. Corrections are appended, not rewritten.
