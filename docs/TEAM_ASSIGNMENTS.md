Ownership means coordinating implementation and integration, not working alone. See the individual brief for each member under `docs/team/`.

## Shared commitments

Every member agrees the common contracts before changing them, adds one normal and one edge/failure case, documents a sample input/output, keeps mock/deterministic mode usable, reviews integration changes affecting their interfaces, and helps present the final demo. Dependencies and handoffs are listed in each brief.
